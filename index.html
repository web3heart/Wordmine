How to play

  • Slide your finger across the letters in the circle to make a word, then lift to submit. Slide back to undo a letter. Any real English word counts.
  • Make a word you have never made before and you get a sparkle sound and a NEW tag. Find the mother word to see what it means.
  • Hit the target number of words before the timer ends to clear the level.
  • The level ends the moment you hit the target. Stars depend on time left: 1 star for clearing it, 2 with 25% of the time left, 3 with 50% left. Finding the mother word (all the letters) guarantees at least 2 stars.
  • Later levels have less time, higher targets and longer minimum words.
  • Hints cost 10 seconds. A new Daily Challenge appears every day.
